**Verdict:** The best fits under 30k EUR are Toyota full hybrids, the C-HR, Yaris Cross and RAV4, with Lexus UX 250h close behind. Kia Sportage HEV and Hyundai Tucson HEV are the best non-Toyota options. Expecting to resell for more than you paid is not realistic if you buy at normal retail. Losing very little is realistic. Gaps in the research: I found no Spanish depreciation figures per model, so every per-model annual % below is my own estimate from listing prices (marked "est."). BYD and Honda data was thin. Corolla Cross and Honda ZR-V are effectively over budget.

## 3. Spain-specific factors

- **ECO label in Madrid:** an ECO car (HEV, or a PHEV with less than 40 km electric range) can drive anywhere in the Madrid ZBE, including Distrito Centro. Zona SER parking is 75% off, with a 2-hour limit in Centro. [RACE](https://www.race.es/zonas-de-bajas-emisiones/mapa-zbe-madrid), [Autopista](https://www.autopista.es/noticias-motor/zbe-madrid-en-2026-normas-restricciones-vehiculos-permitidos-en-sus-zonas-bajas-emisiones_323169_102.html)
  - The planned DGT label reform was dropped. Congress rejected it in May 2026, so labels are unchanged. [RACE](https://www.race.es/nuevas-etiquetas-dgt), [Autopista](https://www.autopista.es/noticias-motor/coches-etiqueta-eco-dgt-como-microhibridos-pueden-perderla-tendran-restricciones_327719_102.html)
  - The risk could come back in a future law (inferred).
  - A CERO label gives no extra practical access in Madrid today over ECO. So paying extra for PHEV or EV just for the label makes little sense.
- **EVs:** a 2021 Model Y has lost about 50% or more, blamed on Tesla's price cuts on new cars. [Híbridos y Eléctricos](https://www.hibridosyelectricos.com/coches/politica-precios-tesla-castiga-model-y-2021-este-coche-electrico-pierde-mas-50-su-valor_87961_102.html)
  - The Model Y is still one of the better EVs for value retention, at about 57 to 62% after 3 years (estimates). [Drivingeco](https://www.drivingeco.com/ranking-2026-los-suv-electricos-con-mejor-valor-de-reventa-estimado-a-tres-anos/)
  - Battery health matters: an EV at 78% battery health is worth about 10 to 12% less than one at 88%. [ahorrove](https://www.ahorrove.es/articulo/pierde-valor-coche-electrico-depreciacion-reventa-2026/)
- **PHEVs:** HEVs lose 8 to 12 points less than the equivalent PHEV after 3 years (estimate). [Highmotor](https://www.highmotor.com/como-elegir-un-suv-con-mejor-valor-de-reventa-en-2026-marcas-motorizaciones-y-2.html)

- **Toyota Relax:** the hybrid battery is covered up to 15 years or 250,000 km, renewed with each yearly service at the official network. It also applies to used cars. [Toyota prensa](https://prensa.toyota.es/toyota-relax/), [Toyota battery warranty](https://www.toyota.es/service-and-accessories/warranty-and-assistance/garantia-baterias-coche-nuevo)

[Kia](https://www.kia.com/es/kia-service/garantia-y-manuales/garantia/), [Caralyze](https://caralyze.es/blog/garantia-hyundai-5-anos-kia-7-anos-coche-importado-europa-espana-2026/)

  - MG ZS Hybrid+ prices seem stable, but only because it is cheap new (about 20 to 23k). There is no Ganvam study; UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Mileage

The data does not show a cliff at 100,000 km.

- AutoUncle Spain: each 25,000 km costs about 6% of value, almost in a straight line. A car at 100 to 125k km is worth about 20% less than one at 0 to 25k km. [Dealcar/AutoUncle summary](https://dealcar.io/blog/cuanto-vale-coche-muchos-kilometros)
- Clear extra discounts start around 150k km for petrol cars. [Dealcar](https://dealcar.io/blog/cuanto-vale-coche-muchos-kilometros)
- What hurts resale most is mileage far above the expected 15 to 20k km a year for the car's age, and a missing service history.
- Toyota and Lexus do tolerate more mileage, which supports your belief:
  - The 15-year / 250k km Relax cover is a concrete reason.
  - Example listings near 100k km: 2022 Corolla with 110k km at 21,000 EUR, 2022 RAV4 with 119k km at 34,000 EUR. [Wallapop](https://es.wallapop.com/coches-segunda-mano/toyota-hibrido)
  - Whether Toyota holds value at high km better than other brands is inferred, not measured.
- For Kia the hard line is 150,000 km, where the transferable warranty ends.
- My rule of thumb: aim for 15 to 20k km per year of age. Toyota and Lexus with full official service can go to about 120 to 150k km. Others are best under about 100k km.

## 6. Can you make money on resale?

Not at retail.

- **Transaction costs:** buying from a dealer includes a 10 to 20% margin (inferred, industry norm). Buying from a private seller in Madrid means paying ITP, a transfer tax on the Hacienda value (rate about 4%, UNVERIFIED), plus DGT transfer fees.
- **Market direction:** the overall market fell in 2026. Electrified cars aged 1 to 3 years rose only about 3 to 5% a year. That rise covers less than the costs above.
- **The realistic best case:** a Toyota HEV loses about 5 to 10% a year.
- **When breaking even or a profit can happen:**
  1. You buy below market: an urgent private sale, an ex-rental or fleet car, or a trade-in bought directly.
  2. A supply shock hits, like the 2021 to 2022 chip shortage.
  3. You sell privately what you bought at trade-in prices.
- **Practical play:** a 2 to 4 year old C-HR, Yaris Cross or RAV4 with full official Toyota service, ideally one owner, at 15 to 20k km per year. Keep the Relax yearly service going and sell privately.
