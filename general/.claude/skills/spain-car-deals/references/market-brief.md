# Spain used hybrid/EV SUV market brief (researched 2026-11-26)

**Verdict:** the two reports agree on the big picture and mostly fit together: Toyota full hybrids (C-HR, Yaris Cross, RAV4 220H) bought from the official Toyota network or a solid dealer, cash price only. I found 3 real errors and 2 internal inconsistencies, listed in section 5. The biggest gap: neither report compared against NEW car prices. A new Yaris Cross costs 27,050 EUR cash and a new C-HR 140H about 28,050 to 29,750 EUR (Toyota promos until 30 Nov), so a used 2024/2025 car near those figures is a bad deal. Stock is real: coches.net Madrid (cash price up to 30k) shows 352 C-HR, 153 Yaris Cross and 104 RAV4 hybrids today.

## 4. Km rules

| Brand group | Max km | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Toyota, Lexus | Up to about 150k km if full official service history (Relax renews to 250k km). Sweet spot under 100k km for easy resale | Relax cover; resale report: no value cliff at 100k km, about 6% value per 25k km |
| Honda | Up to about 120k km with full Honda history (no Relax-type cover; my estimate) | Same e:HEV reliability reputation, but no long warranty to sell on |
| Acura | Not sold in Spain, ignore | - |
| Everyone else (Kia, Hyundai, Nissan, etc.) | **Hard max 100k km** (buyer's rule, stricter than the resale report's 150k for Kia) | Buyer's rule wins |
| All | Avoid more than 20k km per year of age | Hurts resale more than absolute km |

## 5. Red flags and deal checklist

| Step | Check |
|---|---|
| Price | Get the cash price in writing, all fees included (transfer, prep, delivery). Compare against the new-car price for 2024+ cars |
| Financing offers | Ask: APR, total cost, minimum term, early-repayment penalty, and whether the discount is clawed back on early repayment. Reject insurance bundles you can't cancel |
| DGT report (8.67 EUR, miDGT app or sede DGT) | ITV km readings vs odometer, number of owners, charges (embargo, reserva de dominio), renting / rental history, label (ECO), first-registration date |
| Service history | Toyota/Lexus: full official network stamps, ideally yearly (needed for Relax). Ask the dealer to print the history from the plate |
| Hybrid system | Toyota: ask for the official hybrid health check (needed anyway to activate Relax). Others: battery state report from the brand dealer |
| Warranty | Minimum 12 months from a dealer (legal minimum for used). Toyota Ocasion gives 24+ |
| Private sale | Budget 4% ITP on the higher of price or Hacienda table value, plus 55.70 EUR DGT fee. Pay by bank transfer at the moment of signing, never a deposit beforehand |
| Red flags | Km jumps or gaps in ITV history; price far below market with urgency; seller not the registered owner; financed-only price; "Remate" stock with no warranty; ex-rental with 40k+ km per year |

Hidden resale costs neither report covered: when you sell privately, your buyer pays 4% ITP, so private buyers bargain harder; selling to a dealer or trade-in typically gets 15 to 20% less than retail (inferred). Insurance: get quotes before buying, RAV4 and Lexus cost more than Yaris Cross / C-HR (not checked).
